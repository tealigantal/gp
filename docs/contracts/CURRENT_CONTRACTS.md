# Current Contract Kernel

GP has one recommendation lifecycle.

1. `RecommendationPlan` is an immutable decision version from the Adaptive Decision Engine. A base plan binds the session, completed daily evidence, complete candidate universe, policy state, and producer revision. An optional lunch-derived plan may retain that full evaluated scope while adding technical observations only for the base plan's frozen Top-30, preserving every final score and its existing Serenity contribution from one exact, complete 11:30 five-minute batch; it is a new plan and never mutates the base plan.
2. `RuntimeObservation` is immutable intraday execution evidence for exactly one plan. It may only report symbols already evaluated by that plan.
3. `RecommendationPublication` is the only product projection. `PublicationService` alone constructs and commits it from a plan plus an optional runtime observation.

`PlanService` resolves the target before selection. A session before or during trading uses the current session and the latest completed daily evidence. After close, a pending daily bar yields an explicit pending state; a completed daily bar targets the next open session. The base plan identity is independent of polling time. A lunch-derived identity additionally binds the base plan ID and normalized closed five-minute content through the existing producer and policy identity fields.

The lunch batch is atomic: every frozen Top-30 symbol plus CSI300 must contain the exact 24 closed bars from 09:35 through 11:30 for the same session. Collection starts no earlier than 11:32 to avoid accepting an upstream row at the instant its label first appears. Any missing, duplicate, unordered, null, mixed-session or unclosed input means no lunch plan and no current-publication switch. A lunch plan must be published with its 11:30 `RuntimeObservation`; the lunch market gate remains deny. Existing conversation sessions keep their first publication, while a new session may bind the latest lunch publication.

The product database owns `recommendation_plans`, `runtime_observations`, `recommendation_publications`, `current_publication`, `sessions`, `turns`, and `claims`. The separate operational ledger owns frozen raw/expected universe counts, exact-date coverage and per-symbol no-bar evidence; it never becomes a recommendation or chat source. A daily coverage denominator may exclude a symbol only for a fresh target-session all-zero spot row or a verified official suspension fact. A run marked `reconstructed_current_universe` may still consume the former for its current target date: the approximation describes the missed 14:57 denominator capture, not a fresh same-session fact; historical or cross-day reconstructed runs remain ineligible. The latter must bind the exact symbol and target date, a pre-open CNINFO disclosure, a verified exchange listing, a document digest, and PDF text binding the target through an exact date, an explicit finite interval, or bounded continuation (at most 10 trading sessions within a declared limit; five trading sessions for explicit continuation without a limit, counted inclusively from the initial suspension date using the existing versioned local calendar). Provider emptiness is never suspension proof. The complete pre-open disclosure window starts ten trading sessions before the target. Parsed issuer facts from every readable document participate in conflict resolution; an effective resumption on or after the halt start invalidates the carried halt. Same-time or later unresolved status/unknown/correction disclosures block exclusion, while read failures of positively identified ancillary reports do not veto a verified notice. Newer explicit disclosures supersede older incomplete documents. Policy `official-suspension.v3` records the dated fact, inclusive session count, calendar reference, source digest and per-document diagnostics in existing operational evidence JSON. An affirmative dated halt whose immediately coordinated issuer clause resumes after a future disclosure records the resumption condition and trigger family, with the same five-session bound when no duration is declared. Any same-time or later potentially fulfilling disclosure withdraws that carried proof, even without a dated resume sentence; unreadable relevant titles also block. A newer explicit halt is evaluated as new evidence. Failed checks retain their specific reasons alongside provider errors without changing coverage. Missing calendar coverage, late disclosures and ambiguous proof never default to a suspension; historical completed runs and plans are unchanged.

Public reads are `GET /api/recommendation/current`, `GET /api/conversations`, and `GET /api/conversations/{session_id}`. `DELETE /api/conversations/{session_id}` removes exactly one conversation plus its cascaded turns and claims; it never deletes the bound publication, plan, runtime, or another session. Deleted session IDs are tombstoned so a delayed chat request cannot recreate them. The conversation reads expose canonical `ConversationSession` and ordered `ConversationTurn` records; they do not recreate old Book, diagnostic, or run projections. Health reports publication lineage and historical runtime fields, plus two server-clock projections: `market_now` describes the already published plan's current relation and execution permission, while `next_plan_target` describes the independent target session, required daily evidence, recovery coverage and publication state. `ready_to_publish` means the required daily evidence is complete but a new publication does not yet exist; UI and narration must say exactly that and must label any displayed candidates as the prior complete plan. A completed required run may create the next-session base plan from 15:20 through the next open session's 09:30, including an overnight recovery completion. An expired current plan never means that tomorrow's plan is forbidden or absent. The next-plan projection reads the exact required date in the operational ledger; `market_recovery` independently describes the oldest backlog. A completed required date is ready even with an older gap, and an absent exact date never inherits checkpoint readiness. Consumers must not mix one scope's date with another scope's counts or state; the sidebar labels separate historical work independently, and chat receives that independent older-backlog state in its temporal facts. Only these projections may support a current-time or next-session user claim. The LLM may route and narrate only publication-bound evidence; it cannot select, rerank, or change values.

The next-session base-plan window is enforced twice: before full-market generation and immediately before its publication. A calculation that starts before 09:30 but finishes afterward may remain as an immutable unpinned audit plan; it cannot move `current_publication` during trading.

After a daily source is ready, its isolated market-run child continues through every not-yet-attempted 100-symbol batch, persists exact-date coverage, and heartbeats its lease after each batch. There is no whole-market wall-clock cutoff: source request timeouts and bounded routes contain an individual request, while retry is reserved for the unresolved residue after a complete persisted pass. A healthy scan may not be interrupted merely because it has already run for an arbitrary number of minutes. Exact readback remains full-universe evidence, but its query uses the indexed target-date range and the ledger mutates only the batch that was just observed.

`POST /api/chat` is the sole narration entry. Before LLM prose, `ConversationService` computes an answer-time projection from the actual Shanghai clock and the bound publication: current phase, execution permission, plan-session relation, daily-evidence date, plan generation/publication times, latest RuntimeObservation as a historical snapshot, and the independent next-plan target with read-only recovery state. The projection does not mutate a publication. It is the only authority for phrases such as “current”, “can execute”, “post-close”, or “next-session”; LLM prose that conflicts with it is not persisted. The same narration may give a fixed manual tail-entry checklist for the plan's already-selected candidates: this is a user-operated 14:45–14:56 observation protocol, not a runtime signal, automatic execution engine, real-time data claim, candidate rerank, or numerical engine output. Without supplied real-time values, the LLM must tell the user to inspect their own screen and must not claim that VWAP, volume ratio, relative strength, price, or five-minute conditions have already passed. No alternate narrator, legacy tool router, or legacy payload contract may be restored.

Daily producer revision 5 binds committed mature-memory input identity, the frozen scoring-policy digest, and policy `daily_score_v6_smoothed_gain_loss`. The worker validates one complete immutable price snapshot before staging cases, and only atomically completed maintenance batches are readable. Case first-seen and batch completion must precede knowledge time; T+5 outcome availability must precede the evidence day. Historical context is date-truncated breadth of the explicitly bound cohort, not proof of point-in-time historical membership. Provider-only exclusions are revoked with preserved audit evidence.

The unique core score is `(N*G + 0.5*n0*A0)/(N*(G+L)+n0*A0)`. G/L are similarity-weighted unconditional positive/negative three-day returns after the existing 0.003 cost per case. Deduplicate event IDs; group normalized weights by signal date, then `N=(1/sum(W_d**2))*mean(similarity)`. N is evidence support, not probability or independent sample count. `configs/daily_scoring.json` freezes common A0=0.03997941946323374 and initial configurable n0=20, with source/time/cost/digest metadata. All quantities are decimal returns. Invalid required cases or unavailable/nonpositive A0 fail explicitly; valid G=L (including all-zero net outcomes) yields 0.5. Confidence, probabilities and execution quality remain explanatory facts and never add to this score.

Within the unchanged valid scope, freeze Top-30 on core score, apply exactly one `clip(core_score+0.03*alpha,0,1)` for an exact complete Serenity batch, then select at most three priority observations by final score. There are no 40/50/55 score bands or net-edge admission gate. `nonpositive_net_edge` remains a risk fact; ranking does not establish positive edge or execution permission. Existing coverage and execution gates remain. `RankingAssessment` optionally records gain/loss/support/a0/n0/core_score/policy_revision; old nulls mean unrecorded. New producer/policy identities and the frozen policy digest prevent old-plan reuse; historical plans are never recomputed or rewritten. `ranking.score` equals final `adaptive_score`; display is that value times 100.

Lunch producer revision 3 / `lunch_5m_observation_only_v3` requires a new-policy base and preserves scores and the existing announcement contribution exactly. Technical signals are observations with zero ranking weight/contribution; no reranking claim is recorded. Collection completeness, publication atomicity and lunch execution deny are unchanged. See [bounded validation and reference provenance](../smoothed_scoring_20260918.md).

The daily producer still anchors the stop below the entry-zone lower bound and reports reward/risk from the upper-bound entry price. The chat evidence projection carries engine-computed return/cost/net-return percentages and structured net-edge risk facts; the narrator cannot calculate them or confuse modeled friction with actual user costs.
